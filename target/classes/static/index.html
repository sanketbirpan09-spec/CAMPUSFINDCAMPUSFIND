How CampusFind Works

A 3-step community-powered loop to safeguard campus belongings

📝

1. Post a Report

Provide item specifics, date, campus location, contact info, and an optional photograph.

🔎

2. Search & Match

Filter through recent posts or search keywords to identify matching items across campus buildings.

🎉

3. Reunited & Resolved

Connect with the reporter, verify ownership, collect the item, and mark the report resolved.
